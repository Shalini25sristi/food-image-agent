🍛 AI Food Image Agent

Automated pipeline that reads food items from Excel, finds suitable images online, validates quality with computer vision, resizes to 1800×1200 (<10 MB) and uploads them to Google Drive.

Excel→Image Search→Quality Check→ Resize→Rename→Google Drive→Report
